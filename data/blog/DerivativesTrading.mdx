# Derivatives Trading: Overcoming Project Challenges with Strategic Leadership

## Situation

A $30 million project to implement a new **derivatives trading system** was at a standstill. Referred by a colleague, Stephen Wise engaged with the **Head of Funds Strategy & Global Fund Products** in London to assess the stalled project. The joint venture was managing funds for institutional clients, but unknown to the executive, the project team had hit a major roadblock.

Morale was at an all-time low. The specialized nature of the project in **Derivatives Trading** led to high stress among staff, with people quitting, going on sick leave, and requesting transfers. The project seemed on the brink of failure.

## Intervention

Within a few weeks, Stephen recommended a **temporary halt** to the project. This pause allowed time to re-evaluate and rebuild the team. Key actions included:

- Development of job postings to attract new talent.
- Inviting former team members back to the project.
- Requesting additional full-time support from the vendor.
- Adjusting project leadership to align with the team’s needs.

The leadership team was spread across **Toronto, London, Paris, and Luxembourg**, making **cultural awareness** and **communication** critical. Stephen’s coaching of executives before difficult meetings improved accountability and bridged the differing leadership styles across regions.

Three months into the intervention, the project team gained momentum. Key priorities in **Derivatives Workflow**, **Reporting**, **Trade Capture**, **Pricing**, and **Accounting** were identified, and daily meetings kept the team focused and productive. **Co-location** of team members was increased, and **recognition events** were held every two months to boost morale and celebrate progress.

## Results

The revitalized project resulted in the successful implementation of the new derivatives trading system. Key outcomes included:

- **Improved competitive offering** compared to BONY, J.P. Morgan, and State Street.
- **Enhanced reliability of pricing** and trading operations.
- **Improved straight-through processing** capabilities.

The project not only met the original business case goals but also rebuilt a high-functioning team that transitioned smoothly to operational support after the system went live.
